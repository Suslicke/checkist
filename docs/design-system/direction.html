Calm money, one number first

A redesign of the family budget. Home opens on what the family spent this month, or on another main number picked in settings. Amounts read as typography, and red is kept for the brand and the one main action on each screen.

What 1.x gets right, and what holds it back

The engineering underneath is strong. The surface grew one feature at a time, so every block now speaks at the same volume.

Keep

  • Exact money everywhere through one helper, lib/money.ts.
  • Undo instead of «Вы уверены?»: swipe to delete with five seconds to take it back.
  • Skeletons drawn in the shape of the screen that is loading.
  • All colors in one file, with contrast ratios written next to them.
  • Offline queue and screens that open instantly from memory.

Retire

  • The main button is red in the light theme and gold in the dark one, so there are two «main» buttons to learn.
  • Two accents, six category colors, eight chart series and five states compete on one screen.
  • A dark header strip in the light theme: heavy chrome that takes height from content.
  • Home stacks about ten blocks at equal weight, so nothing reads first.
  • Integer, kopecks and ₸ share one weight, and every block is a card, so hierarchy comes from boxes instead of type.

Six principles

Each one follows a pattern that current money apps share, and each ends in a concrete rule for this codebase.

One number first

Good money apps open on one number instead of ten equal blocks. Which number matters is personal, so the person picks it.

In Checkist. Home opens on «Потрачено в ноябре» with the budget bar. A setting swaps it for «Осталось», «Свободно» or «В день».

Money is type

Amounts are the product, so they get the care a headline gets. The integer carries the weight; kopecks and the currency step back. Digits are tabular.

In Checkist. One <Money> component renders parts from lib/money.ts. Amounts stay exact, as decided on 15 October.

Calm until it matters

Spending is normal, so it is drawn in ink. Color appears when something needs a decision, and never as the only signal.

In Checkist. Red is the seal: brand and the one main action. Copper means near or over a limit. Errors are raspberry and always carry words.

Every number shows its source

Trust comes from provenance and reversibility. Each spend says where it came from, and destructive actions can be undone.

In Checkist. A source stamp on every row: Фото, Выписка, Словами, Вручную. Undo stays the pattern for delete.

Thumb first, private in public

Main actions live in the lower third of the screen. One tap hides every amount, for the bus and the office.

In Checkist. A floating tab bar with «＋» on its own. Tap the big number to blur amounts; the device remembers the choice.

AI next to the data

An assistant helps most beside the number it explains, in two sentences, with any change waiting for a person.

In Checkist. Insight cards on Home and in Статистика. Actions still go through confirm, as the chat already does.

Limit with pace

The bar is spent against the limit; the tick is where the month stands today. Ink below 80 %, copper from 80 %, and past 100 % the words «сверх лимита» carry the meaning.

Buttons

One seal button per screen; everything else is tonal or quiet. Danger is an outline in the error color. 48 px tall on phones, 40 px with a mouse. A press shrinks the button to 97 % for 90 ms.

Chips, segments, switch

Selection is ink and action is seal, so a chosen filter never looks like a button to press. Segments switch views of the same data. A switch acts at once; this one hides every amount on the page.

The month

Pace comes first: the plan as a dashed line, the family's spending in ink, today as a dot, and one sentence for screen readers. Categories follow as a single ribbon and a list.

Rollout in six steps

Ordered so each step ships alone and the app stays usable between them. The server changes once: allowance gains a left field for «Осталось», with no migration. Everything else Home needs already comes in base, by_day, attention and /api/money/.

  1. Retune the palette

    New values on the existing names. --ck-primary becomes seal in both themes, --ck-accent becomes the assistant gold, the header strip tokens take page colors, categories get the new tones. No component CSS changes.

    theme/tokens.css

  2. Swap the type

    Geologica and Onest in the font link, JetBrains Mono back for the slip only. New values on --ck-text-*; every size still goes through the scale.

    index.htmltheme/tokens.css

  3. Teach money to read

    lib/money.ts also returns parts and one <Money> renders them. Privacy mode is a class on <html> plus checkist.privacy in localStorage, with «сумма скрыта» for screen readers.

    lib/money.tscomponents/Money.tsx

  4. Float the tab bar

    The bottom menu becomes a glass capsule with «＋» beside it. --ck-bottom-occupied is recalculated, so sticky action bars and the single scroll-padding-bottom keep focus visible.

    src/menu/components/ActionBar.css

  5. Home leads with one number

    By default the main number is base.spent, compared with the same day last month through that month's by_day. The setting «Главное число» in «Ещё» and on «Аккаунт» switches it to «Осталось» (allowance.left), «Свободно» (flow.free from /api/money/) or «В день» (allowance.per_day). It is stored on the device as checkist.homeFigure, like «Значки». Below it come the budget bar, three rows of attention and recent spends; limits, plans and goals fold under «Подробнее о месяце».

    features/dashboard/features/more/budget/allowance.py

  6. Slip and the print

    The receipt page takes the slip styling, grown from the old .ck-tape. The print plays once after recognition, never on later visits.

    features/receipts/features/recognition/

Decisions for you

These change rules written in CLAUDE.md, so they are yours to approve. The right column is the recommendation.

Main number

Today

The top card of Home leads with «можно тратить в день».

2.0

«Потрачено в ноябре» by default. «Главное число» in «Ещё» and on «Аккаунт» switches to «Осталось», «Свободно» or «В день», remembered per device. If it should follow a person across phones, it moves to the profile later.

Brand colors

Today

Red and gold, with a different main color in each theme.

2.0

Still red and gold, retuned. One red for brand and action in both themes; gold moves to the assistant and milestones.

Header

Today

A dark strip with a red line in both themes.

2.0

A page-colored top bar that turns to glass on scroll, and a floating tab bar below.

«＋» placement

Today

In the middle of the bottom menu.

2.0

Its own round seal button beside the tab bar, so navigation and action stay apart.

Fonts

Today

Manrope for everything.

2.0

Geologica for headings and big figures, Onest for interface text, JetBrains Mono only inside the slip. All three have Cyrillic.

Motion

Today

Four animations allowed.

2.0

Nine, listed above. Five are new, and all switch off under reduced motion.

Privacy mode

Today

Not available.

2.0

Tap the big number or flip «Скрывать суммы»; the device remembers it until you tap again.
